EOT Cranes (Electric Overhead Traveling)

A bridge-mounted crane that travels the full length of a runway, for heavier and wider-span material handling than a jib or monorail system covers.

Two Girder Configurations

Built to your customer's load, working area and working height.

Single Girder EOT

One bridge girder carrying the hoist trolley — suited to lighter loads and standard spans, at lower structural cost.

Double Girder EOT

Two parallel girders carrying the trolley between them — used for heavier loads and wider spans where a single girder isn't sufficient.
