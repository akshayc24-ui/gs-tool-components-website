Flagship System

X-Y Friction Free Rail / GS Monorail System

The overhead rail platform that carries a hoist and trolley across a full bay in both X and Y directions — the backbone behind our jib and EOT crane installations.

How It's Built

The system runs on a run rail and bridge rail pair, connected by a hanger kit to the building structure or a floor-mounted frame. A trolley assembly rides the bridge rail (X-axis) while the bridge itself travels the run rail (Y-axis), carrying the hoist to any point in the bay. An air line kit and safety cable keep the pendant and hoist power supply moving with the trolley.

Where It's Installed

The same rail platform adapts to different mounting conditions.

Floor-Mounted Structure

Aluminum X-Y rail on a floor-mounted frame — common for packing areas and light assembly stations.

Overhanging RCC Structure

Steel X-Y rail suspended directly from an overhanging RCC structure, no floor footprint needed.

Pillar-Mounted Structure

Rail mounted on dedicated pillars where the building structure isn't suited to direct suspension.

CNC Loading / Unloading

Aluminum X-Y rail positioned for pick-and-place loading and unloading directly at CNC machines.
