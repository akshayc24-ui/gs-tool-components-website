About GS Tool & Components

A Decade-Plus of In-House Manufacturing

GS Tool & Components has been operating out of Sahibabad, Ghaziabad for more than 10 years, with an in-house facility covering design, manufacturing, assembly, welding and painting. Our engineering team handles everything from the initial layout to erection and commissioning on your site.

We manufacture X-Y Friction Free Rail and Monorail systems, Jib Cranes and EOT Cranes to the load, span and working height you specify, and pair them with electric chain hoists, wire rope hoists, air hoists and balancers sourced from CE-certified partner brands where needed.
